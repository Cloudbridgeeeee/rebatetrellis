# Product

RebateTrellis: Make supplier agreements easier to manage.

AI financial document software that extracts supplier rebate conditions and compares purchase records against contractual requirements.

- Founder and CEO: Femi Odukoya
- Co-founder and CTO: Nneoma Eze
- Industry: Accounting, Procurement, Supply Chain Management
- Customers: Wholesale distributors, procurement teams and retail chains
- Core features: Agreement extraction, purchase reconciliation, eligibility calculations, discrepancy alerts, evidence reports
- MVP: Agreement uploads and staff-reviewed rebate calculations
- Planned AWS: Textract, Bedrock, S3, Lambda, DynamoDB
- Business model: Subscriptions priced by supplier agreement
- Main action: Request a pilot
