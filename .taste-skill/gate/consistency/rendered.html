Clause 4.2 Volume rebate, tier 2
Where the Distributor purchases more than 35,000 cases in any calendar quarter, the Supplier shall pay a rebate of 3.5% of net invoice value.
Returns and damaged goods credited by the Supplier are deducted from the case count.
The rebate is paid by credit note within 45 days of quarter end.

How a RebateTrellis pilot runs

  1. Upload agreements and purchase records

    Send up to ten supplier agreements and 12 months of purchase exports.

  2. Read the rebate report

    Within two weeks you see every rebate term, what you earned and what you missed.

  3. Review and send the claims

    Your accounts team checks each calculation, adds notes and sends the evidence report to the supplier.

What finance and procurement teams ask before a pilot.

Ask about a pilot

Which agreements can it read?

Scanned or digital PDFs and Word files, including schedules and side letters.

Does it send claims to suppliers?

No. Your staff approve each calculation and send the claim themselves.

What does it cost?

A subscription priced by the number of supplier agreements you track. We quote after the pilot.

Which purchase records do you need?

Invoice, return and credit note exports from your ERP or accounting system, as CSV or Excel.

Who sees our agreements?

Only the people you invite. Each customer’s data is stored separately and never shared.
